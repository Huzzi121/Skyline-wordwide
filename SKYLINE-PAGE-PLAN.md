# Skyline Worldwide Transportation
## Page, Feature, Content & Image Plan

_Last updated: November 2026_

## 1. Product direction

Skyline should feel like a private transportation desk for executives, families, events and organizations — not a generic taxi website.

**Visual direction**
- Palette: ivory, graphite, midnight navy and champagne brass.
- Typography: restrained sans-serif navigation/body with an editorial serif display face.
- Layout: spacious editorial grids, large photography, strong horizontal rules, quiet motion and high-contrast CTAs.
- Voice: calm, discreet, precise, punctual and human.
- Primary conversion: **Request a reservation / Book a ride**.
- Secondary conversion: **Call 24/7 dispatch**.

## 4. Booking and operational features

### Reservation flow

1. Select trip type: airport, point-to-point, hourly or group.
2. Enter pickup, destination, date and time.
3. Add flight number when applicable.
4. Select passenger count and vehicle preference.
5. Add traveler/contact details.
6. Add notes: luggage, child seat, accessibility, meet-and-greet.
7. Submit request.
8. Show confirmation state with reference number and dispatch phone.

### Booking UI requirements

- Desktop booking bar on homepage.
- Full reservation form at `/book`.
- Mobile-first stacked form.
- Clear validation and human fallback: “Call dispatch instead.”
- No fake confirmation; connect to a real backend/form destination before launch.

### Client portal requirements

- Secure login.
- Upcoming reservations.
- Past reservations.
- Traveler profiles.
- Saved addresses.
- Billing/statements for approved corporate accounts.
- Account support contact.

### Image rules

- Prefer original brand photography or generated custom images.
- Use one strong image per hero, not collages.
- Include desktop crop and mobile-safe focal point.
- Provide meaningful alt text for every informative image.
- Keep image treatment consistent: warm highlights, deep shadows, subtle grain only when intentional.
- Use local files in production rather than temporary external image URLs.

## 7. SEO and content requirements

Every service, airport, location and fleet page should have:

- Unique page title.
- Unique meta description.
- One clear H1.
- Short editorial intro above the fold.
- Descriptive image alt text.
- Breadcrumbs.
- Internal links to related pages.
- FAQ content where relevant.
- Local/service schema where appropriate.
- Canonical URL.
- Open Graph image.

Recommended title pattern:

`[Service or Location] Chauffeur & Black Car Service | Skyline Worldwide`
